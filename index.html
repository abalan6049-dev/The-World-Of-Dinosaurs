03 • DEEP TIME

THE MESOZOIC ERA

Follow the rise and transformation of dinosaurs across geological time.

252–201 MYA
TRIASSIC

The Beginning

The first dinosaurs appeared during the Triassic Period. They were initially small and relatively uncommon compared with other reptiles.

201–145 MYA
JURASSIC

Age of Giants

Dinosaurs became dominant across many ecosystems. Giant sauropods such as Brachiosaurus and Diplodocus flourished.

145–66 MYA
CRETACEOUS

The Final Chapter

Dinosaurs reached incredible diversity. T. rex, Triceratops and many other species lived during this final period.

66 MYA
EXTINCTION

The End of an Era

A global mass extinction eliminated all non-avian dinosaurs while birds survived.

06 • EVIDENCE FROM THE EARTH

THE FOSSIL RECORD

Fossils are the clues scientists use to reconstruct the ancient world.

🦴

Bones

Fossilized bones reveal anatomy, growth and evolutionary relationships.

👣

Footprints

Trackways can reveal how dinosaurs moved, travelled and sometimes interacted.

🥚

Eggs

Fossil eggs and nests provide evidence about reproduction and parental behavior.

🪶

Feathers

Feather fossils help demonstrate the evolutionary connection between dinosaurs and birds.
